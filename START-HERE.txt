IRONLOG ICON-ONLY UPDATE

Deploy: Drag the ironlog-icons-only folder onto Netlify Deploys for your existing site. Refresh the site. iPhone may require removing and re-adding its home screen shortcut to refresh its cached icon; do not remove while unsynced workouts remain on that device.
